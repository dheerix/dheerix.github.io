Featured case study · Openlane

Guardlane

A production moderation capability delivered across the application, machine-learning, infrastructure, UI, and operational layers.

Problem

Marketplace conversations could contain contact sharing, abusive content, or other policy violations. Manual and reactive moderation could not provide a scalable, consistent operating path.

My ownership

Owned delivery across requirements, backend orchestration, dataset and model development, SageMaker deployment, dashboard integration, human-review tooling, production readiness, and stakeholder alignment.

System design

Prepared and evaluated labeled data for a multi-label DistilBERT classifier. Guardlane combines deterministic regex detection, the English classifier on SageMaker, and an LLM fallback for non-English or unsupported input behind one moderation API.

Production outcome

Deployed a traceable moderation capability with explicit failure handling, telemetry, asynchronously persisted outcomes, and rollback support. A dashboard enables human review and operational feedback; evaluation and reviewed outcomes guide threshold changes and future model refinement.

Engineering lesson

A model endpoint is not a production AI system. Reliability comes from the surrounding contracts, routing, versioning, telemetry, review workflow, and clear ownership of business decisions.

Request path

Marketplace input → language and pattern detection → DistilBERT or LLM route → normalized moderation evidence → product decision → review and feedback

Other selected work

01 / Openlane

Marketplace modernization

Evolving a live automotive marketplace across legacy, serverless, and modern service generations.

Problem
Oracle and Java systems remained central to vehicle, bidding, sales, and dealer workflows; a one-time replacement was not viable.
My ownership
Implemented event routing, foundational cloud-service upgrades, BFF and frontend capabilities, and supported production delivery across multiple services.
Technical decision
Used integration layers, domain event routing, and BFF boundaries as evolutionary bridges between platform generations.
Outcome · lesson
Enabled cloud-native services and modern experiences to coexist with the live platform. Incremental modernization preserves continuity while reducing future friction.

02 / Openlane

AI Upload

An LLM-assisted workflow for processing dealer-provided vehicle content.

Problem
Repetitive, time-sensitive content processing needed useful AI assistance without removing operational review and control.
My ownership
Led significant parts of delivery, including planning, backend and integration changes, frontend support, phased release, production investigation, and coordination with product and engineering.
Technical decision
Integrated AI as an assistive step with validation before marketplace-ready output, keeping judgment in the operating workflow.
Outcome · lesson
Connected AI capability to real dealer operations. Adoption depends on review paths, observability, and shared workflow assumptions—not model novelty.

03 / Holland & Barrett

Enterprise search & product discovery

Commerce search where relevance, promotions, product data, and runtime behavior meet.

Problem
Customers needed reliable discovery across a changing catalog while the platform also supported navigation and complex promotion rules.
My ownership
Implemented OneSearch and Elasticsearch integrations, search APIs, promotion-engine and ANTLR grammar changes, React features, and production fixes.
Technical decision
Kept Elasticsearch query construction and commerce orchestration behind stable service APIs instead of exposing search infrastructure to clients.
Outcome · lesson
Supported customer-facing search, browse, and promotions with maintainable boundaries. Relevance and business rules are architecture concerns, not database queries.
