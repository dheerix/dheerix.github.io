# Competency Map

Use this map to decide whether an activity belongs in the lab. Company emphasis is
a working hypothesis until a job description or interview guidance confirms it.

| Competency | MaintainX | Foci | Valency | Evidence to produce |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| Requirements and system decomposition | High | High | High | Five-minute architecture walkthrough |
| Distributed delivery and idempotency | High | High | High | Failure matrix and retry design |
| Data modelling and consistency | High | Medium | High | State model plus trade-off explanation |
| Search indexing and relevance | High | Low | Medium | Relevance experiment with judged queries |
| Agent orchestration and tool contracts | Medium | High | High | Traceable workflow and failure taxonomy |
| Context engineering | Medium | High | High | Context budget and selection rationale |
| AI evaluation and guardrails | Medium | High | High | Eval set, rubric, and regression result |
| Human-in-the-loop review | Low | Medium | High | Review state machine and escalation rules |
| Observability and production operations | High | High | High | SLOs, signals, and incident walkthrough |
| Coding and testing discipline | High | High | High | Focused implementation with tests |
| Technical communication | High | High | High | Timed mock with concise answers |

## Evidence rule

Reading is input, not evidence. Evidence is a design explanation, tested
experiment, ADR, measured result, or timed mock answer.
