# Engineering Depth

This is the central workspace for the 2026–27 engineering-depth program.

## Architecture
- `architecture/adr/` — generalized architecture decision records
- `architecture/system-studies/` — deep studies of real system patterns
- `architecture/fundamentals/` — concepts uncovered while investigating systems

## AI
- `ai/production-ai/` — production AI architecture and case studies
- `ai/ml/` — applied ML depth
- `ai/agents/` — agentic systems and PlanForge lessons
- `ai/evaluations/` — evaluation design and reliability

## DSA
- `dsa/patterns/` — reusable problem-solving patterns
- `dsa/grind75/` — interview-maintenance exercises

## Field Notes
Short observations from day-to-day engineering that may later become deeper artifacts.

The goal is evidence of engineering reasoning, not accumulation of notes.
