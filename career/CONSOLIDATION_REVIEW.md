# Career Consolidation Review

The legacy repository contained many parallel career surfaces: `career-assets`, `master-profile`, `master-resume`, `resumes`, `linkedin`, `coverletters`, `interviews`, `promotion`, `stories`, `story-bank`, `experience-bank`, and `leadership`.

Most files in those roots were skeletal placeholders or duplicated concepts. The stable model is now:

- `career/profile/`
- `career/resume/`
- `career/interviews/`
- `career/promotion/`
- `career/stories/`
- `career/communication/`
- `career/leadership/`

Substantive existing career files already under `career/` are preserved. New material should use the stable paths above.

## Rule

Do not rebuild the deleted folder taxonomy inside `career/`. Consolidate by purpose and keep canonical artifacts rather than copies.
