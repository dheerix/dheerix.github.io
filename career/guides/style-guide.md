# Writing Style Guide

Good

"Reduced moderation latency by replacing real-time LLM inference with a fine-tuned DistilBERT model deployed on SageMaker."

Bad

"Made AI faster."

---

Good

"Product requested automated moderation after arbitration risk increased due to misleading seller responses."

Bad

"Product wanted AI."

---

Avoid

- buzzwords
- vague adjectives
- marketing language
- unsupported metrics
- "cutting-edge"
- "state-of-the-art"

Prefer

- measurable observations
- trade-offs
- rationale
- architectural thinking
- production lessons
