# Career

This is the presentation layer over engineering capability.

## Profile
Professional identity, LinkedIn material, and the canonical master profile.

## Resume
Canonical resume material and targeted variants.

## Interviews
Interview preparation and reusable interview assets.

## Promotion
Promotion/SDE3 material, including the 2026–27 depth roadmap.

## Stories
Reusable experience and behavioral stories. Prefer one canonical story over parallel story banks.

## Communication
Cover letters and other professional communication artifacts.

## Leadership
Leadership frameworks and notes that are primarily career-development material. Concrete proof of leadership belongs under `evidence/leadership/`.

Engineering study belongs under `engineering/`; demonstrated capability belongs under `evidence/`. Avoid creating new top-level career folders.
