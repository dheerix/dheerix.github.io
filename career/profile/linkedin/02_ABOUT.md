# About

I’m a software engineer with 14+ years of experience building enterprise software across AI, cloud platforms, digital marketplaces, healthcare, commerce, and sustainability.

Over the years, my career has evolved from application development into platform engineering, production AI, and technical leadership. Rather than specializing in a single technology, I intentionally built experience across frontend, backend, cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, and modern software architecture. That breadth allows me to move comfortably from customer-facing product design to backend services, platform modernization, and production operations.

I enjoy solving engineering problems that sit at the intersection of technology and business. Whether modernizing legacy platforms, integrating AI into production systems, or designing scalable backend services, I aim to deliver solutions that are reliable, maintainable, and valuable to the people who use them.

Throughout my career I’ve worked in automotive marketplaces, healthcare, retail commerce, and sustainability platforms, contributing to cloud-native applications, enterprise modernization, search platforms, AI-enabled products, and end-to-end product delivery.

A few principles guide the way I work:

- Technology should enable business strategy.
- Modernization should be incremental, not disruptive.
- Engineering quality compounds over time.
- Cross-functional alignment is as important as architecture.

Today I’m particularly interested in production AI, distributed systems, platform engineering, software architecture, and helping teams deliver better software through thoughtful engineering and clear technical communication.

I’m always happy to connect with engineers, architects, product leaders, and others who enjoy solving meaningful engineering problems.
