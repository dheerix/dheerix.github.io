# Engineering Philosophy

## Principles I Apply Repeatedly

- Prefer measurable evidence over assumptions.
- Modernize incrementally when production risk is high.
- Build reusable platforms rather than one-off features.
- Optimize for maintainability before cleverness.
- Balance business value with technical quality.
- Preserve continuity when evolving a live system.

## How This Shows Up In My Work

These principles appear consistently across my career:

- platform work instead of isolated feature work
- modernization without big-bang rewrites
- production awareness during implementation
- documentation that supports future reuse
- ownership that extends beyond launch

## Technical Profile

I do not define myself by a single language or framework.

I focus on understanding systems deeply enough to choose the right tool for the job.

That approach has allowed me to work effectively across:

- Java and .NET enterprise systems
- React and TypeScript product interfaces
- cloud-native services
- data and event pipelines
- AI-assisted workflows
- search and discovery platforms

I prefer engineering environments where people:

- think independently
- challenge assumptions respectfully
- improve existing systems
- learn continuously
- share knowledge
- prioritize customer outcomes

When approaching a problem, I try to understand:

1. The business outcome
2. The customer impact
3. The current system
4. The technical trade-offs
5. The operational risks
6. The long-term maintenance cost

I prefer evolutionary change when it reduces risk and preserves business continuity.
