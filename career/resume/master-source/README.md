# Master Resume Repository

## Purpose

This repository is the source layer for the resume ecosystem.

It captures the durable career material that feeds the canonical resume output in [../resumes/01_MASTER_RESUME.md](../resumes/01_MASTER_RESUME.md).

The modular sections in this directory are intentionally comprehensive so derived resumes can stay selective without losing fidelity.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Complete employment history
- All significant projects
- Leadership contributions
- Technical expertise
- Business impact
- Professional achievements
- Technologies
- Certifications
- Awards
- Speaking and community activities

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Final resume formatting
- Role-specific tailoring
- Application-specific pruning
- Output-only variants

Those belong in the `resumes/` layer.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Capture everything once.
- Never lose experience because of page limits.
- Optimize for accuracy before brevity.
- Keep business outcomes alongside technical work.
- Update continuously.
- Derive resume variants from the canonical output layer, not from scratch.
